# About Mohit

I'm Mohit, a senior software engineer with nine years of experience building
production backend systems and, more recently, AI agent applications. I care about
systems that are observable, testable, and boring in the best way — predictable under
load and easy for the next person to reason about.

## What I do

I design and ship backend services and data pipelines. My day-to-day is Python and
TypeScript, with a lot of time spent on API design, reliability, and developer
experience. Lately I've been building agentic and retrieval-augmented systems: LangGraph
agents, RAG pipelines, and the evaluation and tracing scaffolding that makes them
trustworthy rather than just demoable.

## How I think about engineering

I optimise for the total cost of ownership, not the cleverness of any single commit. A
feature isn't done when it works on my machine; it's done when it's tested, observable,
documented, and safe to change. I'd rather have a deterministic, debuggable system than a
magical one that fails in ways nobody can explain.
